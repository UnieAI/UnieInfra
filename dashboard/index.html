UnieInfra on 4× MI350P: AgentX vs vLLM and SGLang

At c16, optimized UnieInfra reaches 518 output tok/s — 1.02× vLLM's 506 and 1.38× SGLang's 376 — with a mean TTFT of 565 ms against 4,580 ms and 11,778 ms. Across the ladder it stays ahead of vLLM's official recipe at c1, c4, c16 (1.18× at c1 · 1.01× at c4 · 1.02× at c16), loses at c64 (586 vs 1,159 tok/s) and falls to 4.8 tok/s at c128, where vLLM still holds 325.

The stress series drop think time and carry the merged fixes (larger KV cache, 4K prefill chunks, INT8 prefill all-reduce). Among them coupled draft, sampled keeps T=1.0 sampling as vLLM does; against the best same-kind vLLM stress run it lands at 1.65× at c1 · 0.90× at c4 · 0.92× at c16 · 0.91× at c32 · 1.10× at c64 · 3.16× at c128.
